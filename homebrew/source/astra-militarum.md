# Astra Militarum for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

Oct 4, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This conversion brings the Astra Militarum into The Horus Heresy 2.0, built on top of the official 2.0 Imperial Army lists rather than replacing them. Where a 40k unit already has a 2.0 equivalent in the Solar Auxilia or Imperialis Militia lists, use the official datasheet. This document adds the units that 2.0 does not cover: Storm Troopers and Tempestus Scions, Kasrkin, Commissars, abhumans, regimental infantry, the 40k-only vehicles, and named characters.

It uses 2.0 mechanics only and follows the same conventions as the Ork and Tyranid conversions: the 2.0 Force Organisation chart, datasheet layouts, options tables with right-aligned costs, and a weapon-family armoury. Points start from 7th edition costs, scaled toward official Solar Auxilia values; treat them as playtest values. Rules marked † lean on exact 2.0 wording; the checklist at the end lists them.

### Units to take from the official 2.0 books

Use the official datasheet for these units, adding Regiment (X) and Voice of Command from this document. Check names against your books, since the 2.0 equivalents are listed from memory†.

Everything else, including Scout and Armoured Sentinels, the Chimera, Hellhound family, Hydra, Wyvern, Manticore, Deathstrike, Leman Russ Punisher, Exterminator and Eradicator, and the Rogal Dorn, has a datasheet below. If your books turn out to cover one of them, use the official version instead.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every unit in this document has **Regiment (X)** unless its entry says otherwise. Officers also have **Voice of Command (X)**.

**Voice of Command (X).** At the start of your Shooting phase, a model with this rule may issue up to X orders, each to a different friendly Astra Militarum Infantry unit within 12" (including its own unit). The ordered unit takes a Leadership test using the officer's Leadership; if it passes, the order takes effect until the end of the turn. A unit may receive only one order per turn.

| Order | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Bring It Down! | The unit may re-roll failed to-wound and armour penetration rolls against Vehicles, Dreadnoughts and Monstrous Creatures. |
| First Rank, Fire! Second Rank, Fire! | Lasguns and hot-shot lasguns in the unit fire one additional shot. The unit may not move or Advance this turn. |
| Fix Bayonets! | The unit adds 1 to its Attacks Characteristic in the next Assault phase. |
| Get Back in the Fight! | The unit is no longer Pinned or Falling Back, and may shoot this phase. |
| Move! Move! Move! | Instead of shooting, the unit moves up to D6 plus its Initiative Characteristic in inches. |
| Take Aim! | The unit may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 when shooting. |

**Abhuman.** Ogryns, Bullgryns and Ratlings may not receive orders unless an order is issued by Nork Deddog or an officer within 6".

**Regiment (X).** See Regiments, below.

**Stealth.** If the model has not moved this turn, it cannot be targeted by ranged attacks from more than 12" away.

**Summary Execution.** If a unit containing a Commissar fails a Morale check or Pinning test, the Commissar may execute one model of your choice from the unit (not the Commissar or another character). The unit then counts as having passed the test, and uses the Commissar's Leadership for the rest of the turn.

**Tank Orders (X).** A Tank Commander may issue up to X orders to Astra Militarum vehicles within 12", using the same procedure: **Full Throttle!** (the vehicle may move an extra 6" but may not shoot), **Gunners, Kill on Sight!** (re-roll to-hit rolls of 1), or **Strike and Shroud!** (after shooting, the vehicle counts as obscured until your next turn).

## Advanced Reaction: Covering Fire

Astra Militarum units use the 2.0 core reactions as normal, plus this advanced reaction. It costs one point of the Reaction Allotment like any other reaction†.

**Covering Fire** (Shooting phase). Trigger: an Astra Militarum Infantry unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. Another friendly Astra Militarum Infantry unit within 6" of the target, and in range and line of sight of the attacker, may make a Shooting Attack against the attacking unit after that attack is resolved, as though using Return Fire†.

## Regiments

Every army is drawn from a regiment with its own way of war. Regiments work like Ork Klans and Hive Fleets.

**Regiment (X).** When building an army, choose one regiment for each detachment. Every unit in that detachment gains Regiment (X), where X is the chosen regiment. Ursarkar Creed, Knight Commander Pask and Colour Sergeant Kell may only join a Cadian detachment; Colonel 'Iron Hand' Straken and Sly Marbo may only join a Catachan detachment. Other Unique characters may join any regiment.

**Regiment Advanced Reactions.** Each regiment has its own Advanced Reaction, usable only by units with that Regiment (X). It is available in addition to Covering Fire and costs one point of the Reaction Allotment†.

### Cadian

*The Imperium's model soldiers, trained from childhood to hold the line against the Eye of Terror.*

- **Born Soldiers:** in a turn in which it did not move, the unit may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 with ranged weapons.
- **Veteran Officers:** Cadian officers issue orders to units within 18" instead of 12".

**Advanced Reaction: Fire Discipline** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Cadian Infantry unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. The unit makes a Return Fire Shooting Attack before the enemy attack is resolved, instead of after it.

**Warlord Trait: Master of Command.** The Warlord's Voice of Command (X) is increased by 1.

### Catachan

*Jungle fighters from a death world, hard as their planet and twice as dangerous.*

- **Brutal Strength:** Infantry models add 1 to their Strength in close combat.
- **Jungle Fighters:** Infantry units have Move Through Cover, and Stealth while in area terrain†.

**Advanced Reaction: Ambush** (Movement phase). Trigger: an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of a Catachan Infantry unit that is in cover. The Catachan unit makes a Shooting Attack against the triggering unit.

**Warlord Trait: Lead from the Front.** The Warlord and any unit he joins gain Rage (2) and Furious Charge (1).

### Death Korps of Krieg

*Grim siege soldiers who seek redemption in death, and fight on long after other men would break.*

- **Grim Demeanour:** Infantry units may re-roll failed Morale checks and Pinning tests.
- **Siege Masters:** artillery and Barrage weapons in the detachment may re-roll the scatter dice.

**Advanced Reaction: Death Before Dishonour** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Krieg Infantry unit. Instead of firing Overwatch, the unit gains Counter-Charge (1) until the end of the phase†.

**Warlord Trait: Siege Commander.** Friendly artillery and Barrage weapons with a target within 12" of the Warlord add 1 to their Ballistic Skill.

### Mordian

*Parade-ground perfect soldiers of the Iron Guard, firing in rigid, devastating volleys.*

- **Iron Discipline:** Infantry models add 1 to their Leadership, to a maximum of 10.
- **Parade Drill:** a Mordian unit that receives the First Rank, Fire! Second Rank, Fire! order may still move, but may not Advance.

**Advanced Reaction: Volley Fire** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Mordian Infantry unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. The unit makes a Return Fire Shooting Attack as though it had received the First Rank, Fire! Second Rank, Fire! order.

**Warlord Trait: Parade Ground Master.** Once per game, all orders the Warlord issues in one turn are passed automatically.

### Tallarn

*Desert raiders who strike fast, fight from ambush, and vanish before the enemy can reply.*

- **Desert Raiders:** Infantry and Cavalry units of 15 or fewer models gain the Outflank special rule†.
- **Swift As the Wind:** vehicles and Cavalry add 2 to their Movement Characteristic.

**Advanced Reaction: Strike and Vanish** (Movement phase). Trigger: an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of a Tallarn unit. The Tallarn unit moves up to D6 plus its Initiative Characteristic in inches (vehicles and Cavalry move up to 6"), and must end further from the triggering unit.

**Warlord Trait: Mounted Raider.** The Warlord, any unit he joins, and any vehicle he is embarked on gain Scout.

### Valhallan

*Stubborn ice warriors who fight to the last man, and expect their comrades to do the same.*

- **Endless Ranks:** Infantry units of 20 or more models never become Pinned.
- **Ice Warriors:** Infantry units of 10 or more models have Feel No Pain (6+).

**Advanced Reaction: Hold At All Costs** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Valhallan Infantry unit. The unit fires Overwatch with every model, adding 1 to its Ballistic Skill for the Overwatch, to a maximum of 5†.

**Warlord Trait: Grim Resolve.** Friendly Valhallan units within 12" of the Warlord use his Leadership.

## Warlord Traits

An Astra Militarum Warlord chooses one Warlord Trait: one of the three generic traits below, or the Warlord Trait of his regiment.

### Bellowing Voice

*Orders that carry across the din of battle.*

The Warlord issues orders to units within 18" instead of 12". In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Movement phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

### Grand Strategist

*A commander who plans every battle three moves ahead.*

You may re-roll one Reserves roll each turn. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Shooting phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

### Iron Will

*An officer whose men would rather face the enemy than his displeasure.*

The Warlord's Leadership is 10, and friendly units within 6" of him may re-roll failed Morale checks. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Assault phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

## Psychic Discipline: Sanctioned Psykana

A sanctioned psyker uses each power in the phase it names, making a Psychic check against its Leadership; a failed check causes Perils of the Warp as in the 2.0 rulebook†.

**Psykana (X).** Every sanctioned psyker knows the Psychic Maelstrom psychic weapon, plus X psychic powers from the table below, chosen when the army is built.

## Force Organisation and Rites of War

Use the 2.0 Crusade Force Organisation chart: HQ, Elites, Troops, Fast Attack, Heavy Support, Dedicated Transports and Lord of War. Official Solar Auxilia and Militia units take their usual slots; the new units in this document take these:

| 2.0 slot | New Astra Militarum units |
| --- | --- |
| HQ | Lord Commissar, Primaris Psyker, Tempestor Prime, Ministorum Priest, Ursarkar E. Creed, Commissar Yarrick, Colonel 'Iron Hand' Straken, Lord Solar Leontus |
| Elites | Commissar, Kasrkin, Tempestus Scions, Tempestus Command Squad, Veteran Guardsmen, Ogryns, Bullgryns, Nork Deddog, Ratlings, Astropath, Master of Ordnance, Colour Sergeant Kell, Sly Marbo |
| Troops | Infantry Squad, Conscripts |
| Dedicated Transports | Chimera, Taurox, Taurox Prime |
| Fast Attack | Rough Riders, Death Riders, Scout Sentinels, Armoured Sentinels, Hellhound, Valkyrie, Vendetta |
| Heavy Support | Heavy Weapons Squad, Field Ordnance Battery, Hydra, Wyvern, Manticore, Deathstrike, Leman Russ Punisher, Exterminator or Eradicator, Rogal Dorn Battle Tank |

An Astra Militarum army may take one Rite of War, chosen when the army is built. Each has a benefit and a limitation.

**Armoured Company.** Requires a Leman Russ Command tank with Tank Orders as Warlord. Benefit: Leman Russ Strike Squadrons may be taken as Troops and gain the Line sub-type†. Limitation: the army may not include more Infantry units than it has vehicle units.

**Mechanised Infantry.** Requires an officer with Voice of Command as Warlord. Benefit: any Infantry unit of 10 or fewer models may take a Chimera as a Dedicated Transport, and units embarked on a Chimera may receive orders. Limitation: every Infantry unit must start the game embarked on a transport.

**Siege Artillery.** Requires a Master of Ordnance or Lord Commissar as Warlord. Benefit: Heavy Weapons Squads and Field Ordnance Batteries may be taken as Troops, and artillery and Barrage weapons may re-roll scatter dice. Limitation: no Fast Attack choices.

**Tempestus Drop Force.** Requires a Tempestor Prime as Warlord. Benefit: Tempestus Scions may be taken as Troops and gain the Line sub-type, and may arrive by Deep Strike from the first game turn†. Limitation: no Heavy Support choices other than Valkyries or Vendettas taken as transports, and no Lords of War.

## Wargear

Wargear that already exists in 2.0 (frag and krak grenades, melta bombs, smoke launchers, hunter-killer missiles, dozer blades) uses its official rules.

### Brute Shield

*A heavy shield with a crackling energy field, carried by Bullgryns.*

A model with a brute shield has a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

### Camo Cloaks

*Light-bending cloaks worn by forward sentries.*

A unit wearing camo cloaks gains Stealth and Infiltrate.

### Carapace Armour

*Rigid plates worn by Scions and veteran troopers.*

Carapace armour confers a 4+ Armour Save.

### Command Rod

*A badge of office that lets a Tempestor Prime coordinate his forces.*

A Tempestor Prime with a command rod has Voice of Command (3).

### Flak Armour

*Layered padding issued by the trillion to the Imperial Guard.*

Flak armour confers a 5+ Armour Save.

### Frag Bombs

*Grenades sized for Ogryn hands.*

Frag bombs count as frag grenades†.

### Medi-pack

*Field surgery gear and stimms carried by a squad medic.*

A unit that includes a model with a medi-pack has Feel No Pain (6+).

### Platoon Standard

*A banner that reminds the men what they fight for.*

A unit that includes a model with a platoon standard may re-roll failed Morale checks.

### Refractor Field

*A personal field generator issued to officers.*

A model with a refractor field has a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

### Rosarius

*A holy amulet that projects a protective field.*

A model with a rosarius has a 4+ Invulnerable Save.

### Slabshield

*A slab of armour plate wielded like a door.*

A model with a slabshield adds 2 to its Armour Save, to a best of 2+.

### Track Guards

*Armoured skirts that protect a tank's drive systems.*

A vehicle with track guards may ignore the first Immobilised result it suffers on the Vehicle Damage table†.

### Vox-caster

*A backpack radio that keeps the squad in contact with command.*

A unit that includes a model with a vox-caster may re-roll the Leadership test for orders.
