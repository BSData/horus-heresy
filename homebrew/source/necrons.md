# Necrons for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

2026-10-05 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This is a homebrew conversion of the 7th edition Codex: Necrons (2015) range for The Horus Heresy 2.0, plus the units added in 8th to 10th edition 40k that did not exist in 7th edition. 2.0 has no official Necrons, so every unit has a datasheet here. It uses 2.0 mechanics only and follows the same conventions as the other conversions.

The design goals: the Necrons are a slow, relentless army of undying machines. Their infantry is tough and hard to kill for good, their gauss weapons flay any target apart molecule by molecule, and their vehicles repair themselves. They are rarely fast, rarely subtle, and almost impossible to grind down.

**Unit types:** Lokhust and Skorpekh Destroyers, Canoptek Spyders and Reanimators are Infantry with the Mechanised sub-type. The Triarch Stalker and Canoptek Doomstalker are Dreadnoughts. The C'tan Shards are Infantry with the Monstrous sub-type. Necron vehicles use the 2.0 vehicle profile, and the Obelisk and Tesseract Vault are super-heavy vehicles.

Points start from 7th edition costs and are scaled to official 2.0 Legion values; treat them as playtest values. Base sizes follow current kits. Rules marked † lean on exact 2.0 wording; the checklist at the end lists them.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every unit in this document has **Dynasty (X)** and **Reanimation Protocols (5+)** unless its entry says otherwise. Vehicles have **Living Metal** instead of Reanimation Protocols.

**Ever-living.** A character with this rule may use Reanimation Protocols even if every other model in its unit has been destroyed.

**Gauss.** A weapon with the Gauss trait has Breaching (6+), and each armour penetration roll of 6 it makes always causes at least a glancing hit, regardless of the target's Armour Value†.

**Living Metal.** A vehicle with this rule ignores Crew Shaken results on the Vehicle Damage table, and at the end of each of your turns, rolls a D6; on a 6, it restores one lost Hull Point†.

**Reanimation Protocols (X).** Each time a model with this rule would lose its last Wound, roll a D6; on a roll of X or more, it is not removed and stays in play with 1 Wound. Reanimation Protocols is not Feel No Pain: rules that ignore or negate Feel No Pain do not affect it. It is a Damage Mitigation roll: a model may make only a single Damage Mitigation roll of any type (including Feel No Pain) for any given Wound. It cannot be used against Instant Death or Destroyer attacks.

**Supersonic.** When a model with this rule moves Flat Out, it must move at least 18" and may move up to 36". It may make a single pivot of up to 90 degrees at the start of its move, and must then move in a straight line. It may pivot and move over enemy models during its move, but may not end its move on top of an enemy model.

**Tesla.** Each to-hit roll of 6 made with a weapon with the Tesla trait inflicts two additional hits on the target.

**Unstoppable.** Necron units never become Pinned, but may not Advance.

## Advanced Reaction: Reanimating Phalanx

Necron units use the 2.0 core reactions as normal, plus this advanced reaction. It costs one point of the Reaction Allotment like any other reaction†.

**Reanimating Phalanx** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Necron Infantry unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. Against that attack, the unit improves its Reanimation Protocols by 1, to a best of 4+, and after the attack is resolved it may make a Return Fire Shooting Attack.

## Dynasties

Every Necron army is drawn from one of the great dynasties of the Necrontyr. Dynasties work like Ork Klans and Sororitas Orders.

**Dynasty (X).** When building an army, choose one Dynasty for each detachment. Every unit in that detachment gains Dynasty (X). Imotekh, Zahndrekh and Obyron may only join a Sautekh detachment; Trazyn and Szeras may join any Dynasty; Orikan only Sautekh; Anrakyr any Dynasty. Each Dynasty's Advanced Reaction is available in addition to Reanimating Phalanx and costs one point of the Reaction Allotment†.

### Mephrit

_A dynasty of solar fire, whose weapons burn hotter than any other._

Units with Dynasty (Mephrit) gain the following:

- **Burning Hatred:** the unit has Hatred (Everything).
- **Solar Fury:** against targets within half range, the unit's ranged weapons improve their AP by 1, to a best of AP 2.

**Advanced Reaction: Solar Flare** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Mephrit unit. The unit fires Overwatch with all ranged weapons, adding 1 to its Ballistic Skill.

**Warlord Trait: Merciless Tyrant.** The Warlord's ranged weapons gain Shred.

### Nephrekh

_A dynasty that has transmuted its bodies to living gold, able to dissolve into light._

Units with Dynasty (Nephrekh) gain the following:

- **Gilded Bodies:** the unit has a 6+ Invulnerable Save, or improves an existing one by 1, to a best of 4+.
- **Translocation Beams:** the unit ignores terrain while moving.

**Advanced Reaction: Phase Shift** (Movement phase). Trigger: an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of a Nephrekh unit. The unit moves up to 6" in any direction, ignoring terrain and models.

**Warlord Trait: Skin of Living Gold.** The Warlord has Feel No Pain (5+).

### Nihilakh

_A proud dynasty of hoarders who never surrender ground they hold._

Units with Dynasty (Nihilakh) gain the following:

- **Aggressively Territorial:** in a turn in which it did not move, the unit may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1.
- **Hold What Is Ours:** the unit improves its Reanimation Protocols by 1, to a best of 4+, while holding an objective.

**Advanced Reaction: Defend the Hoard** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Nihilakh unit. The unit fires Overwatch with every model, and the charger subtracts 2 from its charge distance.

**Warlord Trait: Precognitive Strike.** The Warlord strikes first in close combat, regardless of Initiative†.

### Novokh

_A martial dynasty whose warriors remember the old ways of blade and honour._

Units with Dynasty (Novokh) gain the following:

- **Awakened by Murder:** the unit adds 1 to its Attacks in a turn in which it charged.
- **Blood-fury:** the unit may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 in close combat.

**Advanced Reaction: Crimson Haze** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Novokh unit. Instead of firing Overwatch, the unit gains Counter-Charge (1) until the end of the phase†.

**Warlord Trait: Honourable Combatant.** The Warlord's melee weapons gain Rending (6+).

### Sautekh

_The dynasty of Imotekh the Stormlord, relentless conquerors who advance under a storm of fire._

Units with Dynasty (Sautekh) gain the following:

- **Relentless Advance:** the unit may move and fire Heavy weapons as though it had not moved.
- **Stormlord's Command:** the unit may re-roll failed Morale checks.

**Advanced Reaction: Unbroken Line** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Sautekh unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. The unit makes a Return Fire Shooting Attack before the enemy attack is resolved.

**Warlord Trait: Implacable Conqueror.** Friendly Sautekh units within 12" of the Warlord may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1.

### Szarekhan

_The dynasty of the Silent King, cold and calculating, wardens of the Necron empire._

Units with Dynasty (Szarekhan) gain the following:

- **Aegis of the Silent King:** enemy psykers within 12" subtract 1 from their Leadership for Psychic checks.
- **Undying Warriors:** the unit has Reanimation Protocols (4+) instead of (5+).

**Advanced Reaction: Dimensional Shift** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Szarekhan unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. Against that attack, the unit gains Shrouded (5+).

**Warlord Trait: The Triarch's Will.** Friendly Szarekhan units within 12" of the Warlord use his Leadership and auto-pass Morale checks.

## Warlord Traits

A Necron Warlord chooses one of the three generic traits below, or the Warlord Trait of his Dynasty.

### Enduring Will

_A lord whose will to rule outlasts his own body._

The Warlord has Reanimation Protocols (4+), or improves an existing one by 1, to a best of 3+. In addition, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Assault phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

### Eternal Madness

_A noble gripped by the madness of eternity, who fights with mindless fury._

The Warlord and any unit he joins gain Rage (2). In addition, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Movement phase†.

### Phaeron of the Stars

_A strategist who has commanded legions for sixty million years._

You may re-roll one Reserves roll each turn. In addition, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Shooting phase†.

A Necron army may take one Rite of War:

**Canoptek Harvest.** Requires a Cryptek, Technomancer or Plasmancer as Warlord. Benefit: Canoptek Wraiths and Canoptek Scarabs may be taken as Troops and gain the Line sub-type. Limitation: no Lords of War, and Infantry other than Canoptek units must not outnumber Canoptek units.

**Destroyer Cult.** Requires a Destroyer Lord or Skorpekh Lord as Warlord. Benefit: Destroyers, Heavy Destroyers, Skorpekh and Ophydian Destroyers gain Rage (2), and Destroyers may be taken as Troops. Limitation: no Necron Warriors, and no Ghost Arks.

**Royal Court.** Requires an Overlord as Warlord. Benefit: Lychguard may be taken as Troops and gain the Line sub-type, and the Overlord may take a Royal Warden and up to two Crypteks without using HQ slots. Limitation: no Destroyers of any kind.

**Tomb World Awakening.** Requires any Necron HQ as Warlord. Benefit: every unit improves its Reanimation Protocols by 1, to a best of 4+, in the first two game turns. Limitation: no Fast Attack choices, and every unit must deploy on the battlefield (no Reserves).

## Wargear

Every Necron model's armour is part of its living metal body; its Armour Save is shown on its profile.

### Bloodswarm Nanoscarabs

_A swarm of tiny scarabs that mark a target for destruction._

Once per game, one friendly unit arriving by Deep Strike within 12" of an enemy unit Imotekh can see does not scatter.

### Canoptek Cloak

_A shroud of Canoptek wings and repair arms._

The bearer becomes Infantry (Mechanised) with M10 and, if its datasheet says so, Battlesmith (5+)†.

### Dispersion Shield

_A shield that deflects incoming fire back at its source._

The bearer has a 4+ Invulnerable Save. Each time it passes an Invulnerable Save against a ranged weapon on a 6, the attacking unit suffers a hit at that weapon's Strength and AP.

### Empathic Obliterator

_Trazyn's staff, which cuts through foes and their kin alike._

See the Armoury.

### Ghostwalk Mantle

_Obyron's dimensional cloak, bound to his master's location._

As Vargard Obyron's datasheet states.

### Gravity Displacement Pack

_An anti-gravity device carried by the Triarch Praetorians._

A gravity displacement pack counts as a jump pack, using the official 2.0 Jump Pack rules.

### Necrodermis

_The living metal shell that holds a C'tan Shard's power in check._

The bearer has a 4+ Invulnerable Save and Feel No Pain (5+).

### Phase Shifter

_A device that shifts its bearer partly out of phase with reality._

The bearer has a 4+ Invulnerable Save.

### Phylactery

_A small crystal reliquary that holds a spark of the bearer's reanimation._

The bearer improves its Reanimation Protocols by 1 for itself only.

### Resurrection Orb

_A glowing orb that floods nearby Necrons with reanimating energy._

Once per game, at the start of any phase, the bearer's unit may improve its Reanimation Protocols by 2, to a best of 3+, until the end of that phase.

### Sempiternal Weave

_Woven mesh of living metal laced through the bearer's body._

The bearer adds 1 to its Armour Save, to a best of 2+.

### Szarekh's Command Barge

_The Silent King's throne, borne aloft on a floating barge of living metal._

Szarekh's command barge works as a Catacomb Command Barge: Szarekh becomes Cavalry (Mechanised, Unique) with a Movement Characteristic of 14 and Hammer of Wrath (2), and the barge carries a twin-linked tesla destructor or a gauss cannon. He keeps his own Toughness, Wounds and Save.

### Translocation Shroud

_A dimensional cloak that lets its wearer step between places._

The bearer and the unit it joins have Deep Strike. Once per game, at the start of your Movement phase, the unit may be removed and placed anywhere on the battlefield, as though arriving by Deep Strike.

## Rules Checklist (†)

These entries rely on 2.0 wording that should be confirmed. Where your book differs, keep the intent and use the book's mechanic.

- Reanimation Protocols as a Damage Mitigation roll (one per Wound), and its interaction with Instant Death and Destroyer.
- Living Metal, Gauss glancing hits, and the Vehicle Damage table (Crew Shaken, Immobilised, Explodes!).
- Reaction Allotment, Return Fire, Counter-Charge, striking first, and free reactions (Hexmark, Doomstalker, Szarekh, Deathmarks).
- Look Out, Sir! and bodyguard rules (Lychguard, Cryptothralls, Menhirs).
- Battlesmith on Necron vehicles and Mechanised models.
- Antigrav, Flyer, Supersonic, Open-topped and Super-heavy vehicle rules, and disembarking by invasion beam.
- Fear (X), Sniper, Haywire, Jink and the Monstrous and Mechanised sub-types.
- Weapon special rules: Breaching, Rending, Armourbane, Sunder, Shred, Concussive, Pinning, Blast and Ordnance.
