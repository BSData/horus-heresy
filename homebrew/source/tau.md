# T'au Empire for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

2026-10-04 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This is a homebrew conversion of the 7th edition Codex: Tau Empire (2015) range, plus the T'au units added in 8th to 10th edition 40k, for The Horus Heresy 2.0. 2.0 has no official T'au, so every unit has a datasheet here. It uses 2.0 mechanics only and follows the same conventions as the other conversions: the 2.0 Force Organisation chart, datasheet layouts, options tables with right-aligned costs, and a weapon-family armoury.

The design goals: the T'au win with firepower and coordination, not close combat. Their infantry carry the best rifles on the table but are poor fighters (WS 2). Markerlights and drones make every gun more accurate, and units cover one another with supporting fire. Battlesuits give them mobile, heavily armed elites, and Kroot auxiliaries supply the close-combat muscle the T'au lack.

Drones get their own section with full statlines. Every unit that can carry drones adds them as models with those profiles.

Battlesuits are Infantry with the Heavy sub-type, with Jet Packs (Crisis, Stealth) or without them (Broadside), heavy battlesuits (Riptide, Ghostkeel, Y'vahra, R'varna), or Knight-type walkers (Stormsurge). Points start from 7th edition costs and are adjusted where 2.0 changes a unit's value; treat them as playtest values. Base sizes follow current kits and are approximate. Rules marked † lean on exact 2.0 wording; the checklist at the end lists them.

## Army-wide Rules

### Types

#### Battlesuits (type)

Every T'au battlesuit of the Infantry unit type (Commanders, the battlesuit characters, and Crisis, Stealth, XV9 Hazard and Broadside battlesuits) has the Heavy and Mechanised sub-types, using the official 2.0 Mechanised rules. They may fire all their weapons in each Shooting Attack, including Heavy and Ordnance weapons after moving, and may still charge; Poisoned and Fleshbane wounds against them must be re-rolled, but Armourbane weapons re-roll failed To Wound rolls against them; Haywire, Detonation and Battlesmith affect them as Dreadnoughts; and no model that is not Mechanised may join their unit.

#### Drone (sub-type)

Models with the Drone sub-type represent semi-autonomous mechanical constructs designed to accompany and assist sentient operators on the battlefield. A model with the Drone sub-type follows these rules:

- **Controlled Protocol:** If all non-Drone models in the parent unit are destroyed, any remaining models with the Drone sub-type in that unit are immediately removed from play as casualties.
- **Cybernetic Cohort:** Drones may be taken as their own units, or purchased as upgrades for a parent unit with the Drone Controller special rule. Drones taken as upgrades are treated as additional models within that squad. When allocating hits to the unit, wounds may be allocated to any Drone model using its own Toughness and Save characteristics.
- **Inbuilt Protocols:** All models with the Drone sub-type possess Jet Packs and the Defender special rule.
- **Networked Morale:** Models with this sub-type ignore their own Leadership characteristic. Instead, they always use the highest Leadership characteristic available in their parent unit for all Morale checks, Pinning tests, and Regroup tests. A unit made up only of Drones uses its own Leadership.
- **Objective Denial:** Models with this sub-type can never control objectives, but they can deny (contest) objectives controlled by the enemy.

### Special Rules

Every T'au unit in this document has **Sept (X)** unless its entry says otherwise. Kroot and Vespid units have **Auxiliaries** instead.

#### Auxiliaries

Kroot and Vespid fight for the Greater Good, but not as T'au. They do not gain Sept (X) traits, never benefit from Ethereal Invocations, and may not use Supporting Fire. Kroot units have Move Through Cover and Infiltrate unless their entry says otherwise.

#### Drone Controller

A unit with this special rule may include models with the Drone sub-type, either as part of its Unit Composition or bought from its Options, up to the number its datasheet allows. These drones follow the Drone (sub-type) rules for drones taken as part of a parent unit.

#### Independent Character

Mighty heroes go where they are needed, being at the forefront of the most vital charges and leading their troops to victory.

Independent Characters can join other units. They cannot, however, join units that contain Vehicles, Dreadnoughts, Automata or any model with the Monstrous sub-type (unless the Independent Character also has that Unit Type or sub-type). They can join other Independent Characters though to form a powerful multi-character unit.

#### Jet Packs

T'au battlesuits, drones and hover drones use the official 2.0 Jet Pack rules. Vespid use Vespid Wings instead (see Wargear). A unit entirely equipped with jet packs may increase its Movement Characteristic by 6, move over friendly and enemy models and units, and ignore terrain while moving in the Movement phase (Dangerous Terrain tests still apply when beginning or ending a move in it, and Difficult Terrain counts as Dangerous). After it has finished shooting in the Shooting phase, it may make an additional 6" move in the same way, whatever weapons it fired. During any Reaction that lets it move, the move is increased by 6". Models with jet packs gain Bulky (2), or Bulky (3) if they already have Bulky (2), and Deep Strike. T'au battlesuits do not gain Bulky this way; they use the Bulky value on their datasheet instead.

#### Markerlights

When a model fires a markerlight, each hit places one Markerlight token on the target unit instead of causing damage. Tokens last until the end of the turn. Before another friendly unit shoots at the marked unit, it may spend tokens as follows (each token once):

| Tokens | Effect for the firing unit |
|---|---|
| 1 | Re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 |
| 2 | Add 1 to Ballistic Skill (to a max of 5) |
| 3 | The target does not receive cover saves against this attack |

A seeker missile may instead spend one token to fire at Ballistic Skill 5, without line of sight to the target.

#### Saviour Protocols

When a wound is allocated to a friendly T'au character or battlesuit within 3" of a friendly Drone, you may roll a D6; on a 2+, that wound is allocated to the Drone instead (use the Drone's save)†.

#### Sept (X)

When building an army, choose one Sept for each detachment. Every T'au unit in that detachment (not Auxiliaries) gains Sept (X), where X is the chosen Sept. Commander Farsight and his Bodyguard may only join a Farsight Enclaves detachment; Commander Shadowsun, Aun'Va and Longstrike may only join a T'au Sept detachment. Other Unique characters may join any Sept.

#### Stealth

If the model has not moved this turn, it cannot be targeted by ranged attacks from more than 12" away.

#### Tidewall Mobility

- **The Movement Mechanic:** In the Movement phase, if a qualifying T'au unit is entirely on top of the Tidewall section, the piece of terrain itself can be moved up to 6". Unlike regular vehicles, it can drift or glide in any direction to reposition your battle line or glide closer to an objective.
- **Shooting Privileges:** Models occupying the moving Tidewall are treated as Stationary during the following Shooting phase. Fire Warriors or Pathfinders may move up to 6" along with their bunker and still fire their weapons at full efficiency (Rapid Fire weapons at maximum range, Heavy weapons without Snap Shots).

### Advanced Reactions

#### Supporting Fire

T'au units use the 2.0 core reactions as normal, plus this advanced reaction. It costs one point of the Reaction Allotment like any other reaction†.

**Supporting Fire** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a T'au unit. Up to one other friendly T'au unit (not Auxiliaries) within 6" of the charged unit fires Overwatch at the charging unit, as well as the charged unit†.

### Septs

Every T'au force is drawn from a sept world, each with its own approach to the Greater Good. Septs work like Ork Klans and Drukhari Obsessions.

**Sept (X).** When building an army, choose one Sept for each detachment. Every T'au unit in that detachment (not Auxiliaries) gains Sept (X), where X is the chosen Sept. Commander Farsight and his Bodyguard may only join a Farsight Enclaves detachment; Commander Shadowsun, Aun'Va and Longstrike may only join a T'au Sept detachment. Other Unique characters may join any Sept.

**Sept Advanced Reactions.** Each Sept has its own Advanced Reaction, usable only by units with that Sept (X). It is available in addition to Supporting Fire and costs one point of the Reaction Allotment†.

#### Bork'an

A sept of scientists and engineers who field the most advanced weapons in the Empire:

- **Sensor Specialists:** markerlights add one extra Markerlight token for each hit.
- **Superior Craftsmanship:** add 6" to the range of Heavy weapons on battlesuits and vehicles.

**Advanced Reaction: Prototype Shields** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Bork'an battlesuit or vehicle is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. Against that attack, the unit gains a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

**Warlord Trait: Master Engineer.** One weapon carried by the Warlord gains Master-crafted. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Shooting phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

#### Dal'yth

A sept of resilient defenders who use the terrain like no other:

- **Adaptive Camouflage:** the unit has Shrouded (6+) in a turn in which it did not move.
- **Hold the Line:** Infantry may re-roll failed Morale checks while in cover.

**Advanced Reaction: Fall Back and Fire** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Dal'yth unit. Instead of firing Overwatch, the unit moves up to D6 plus its Initiative Characteristic in inches directly away from the charging unit.

**Warlord Trait: Unbreakable Defender.** The Warlord and any unit he joins gain Shrouded (5+) while in cover. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Shooting phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

#### Farsight Enclaves

Breakaway warriors who embrace close-quarters battle in a way the rest of the Empire abhors:

- **Devastating Counterstrike:** the unit may re-roll to-wound rolls of 1 against targets within 12".
- **Way of the Short Blade:** Infantry and battlesuits add 1 to their Weapon Skill, to a maximum of 4.

**Advanced Reaction: Hammer Strike** (Movement phase). Trigger: an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of a Farsight Enclaves battlesuit unit. The battlesuits move up to 6" toward the triggering unit; if this brings them within 1", they count as having charged and fight in the next Assault phase†.

**Warlord Trait: Martial Mastery.** The Warlord adds 1 to his Attacks, and his melee weapons gain Rending (6+). In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Assault phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

#### Sa'cea

A crowded sept of disciplined, methodical soldiers:

- **Calm Discipline:** the unit may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 in a turn in which it did not move.
- **Superior Training:** the unit adds 1 to its Ballistic Skill when firing Overwatch, to a maximum of 5.

**Advanced Reaction: Steady Volleys** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Sa'cea unit. The unit fires Overwatch with every model, and the charger subtracts 2 from its charge distance roll.

**Warlord Trait: Disciplined Commander.** Friendly Sa'cea units within 12" of the Warlord use his Leadership. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Assault phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

#### T'au Sept

The first and greatest sept, disciplined masters of combined fire:

- **Cadre Discipline:** the unit adds 1 to its Leadership, to a maximum of 10.
- **Coordinated Fire:** when using Supporting Fire, up to two other units may fire Overwatch instead of one.

**Advanced Reaction: Mont'ka Strike** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a T'au Sept unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. The unit makes a Return Fire Shooting Attack before the enemy attack is resolved, instead of after it.

**Warlord Trait: Master of the Hunter Cadre.** Friendly T'au units within 12" of the Warlord may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 against units with at least one Markerlight token. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Shooting phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

#### Vior'la

A fiery sept whose warriors favour aggressive, close-range firepower:

- **Hot-blooded:** Rapid Fire weapons fire one extra shot at targets within half range.
- **Strike Fast:** the unit may Advance and still shoot Rapid Fire and Assault weapons in the same turn†.

**Advanced Reaction: Burning Charge** (Movement phase). Trigger: an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of a Vior'la unit. The Vior'la unit makes a Shooting Attack against the triggering unit, using only Rapid Fire and Assault weapons.

**Warlord Trait: Fiery Commander.** The Warlord and any unit he joins may re-roll to-wound rolls of 1 with Rapid Fire weapons. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Movement phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

## Warlord Traits

A T'au Warlord chooses one Warlord Trait: one of the three generic traits below, or the Warlord Trait of his Sept. An Auxiliary Warlord (such as a Kroot Shaper) may only choose a generic trait.

#### Exemplar of the Kauyon

A patient hunter who lets the enemy walk into the trap.

You may re-roll one Reserves roll each turn. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Movement phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

#### Precision of the Hunter

A commander who sees the whole battle as a single firing solution.

Friendly units within 12" of the Warlord gain one extra Markerlight token's effect when they spend tokens. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Shooting phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

#### Through Unity, Devastation

A leader who coordinates every gun under his command.

When a friendly unit within 12" of the Warlord uses Supporting Fire, one additional unit may fire Overwatch. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Assault phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

### Ethereal Invocations

Ethereals are not psykers; their power comes from the total devotion of the T'au. At the start of your Movement phase, each Ethereal may make one invocation. It takes a Leadership test; if it passes, the invocation affects every friendly T'au unit (not Auxiliaries) within 12" until the start of your next turn. Aun'Va may make two invocations each turn.

| Invocation | Effect |
|---|---|
| Calm of Tides | Units automatically pass Morale checks and Pinning tests |
| Sense of Stone | Units have Feel No Pain (6+), or improve an existing Feel No Pain by 1 |
| Storm of Fire | Rapid Fire weapons fire one extra shot at targets within half range |
| Zephyr's Grace | Units may Advance and still shoot in the same turn |

**Failed Invocations.** If the Leadership test fails, the invocation has no effect, and the Ethereal may not invoke again until your next turn.

## Rites of War

A T'au army may take one Rite of War, chosen when the army is built. Each has a benefit and a limitation.

#### Hunter Cadre

Requires an Ethereal or Cadre Fireblade as Warlord. Benefit: every Strike Team and Breacher Team may include one Marker Drone without counting toward its drone limit, and Supporting Fire may be used by one additional unit. Limitation: no Lords of War, and Infantry must outnumber battlesuits.

#### Kauyon

Requires a Commander or Cadre Fireblade as Warlord. Benefit: Pathfinders may be taken as Troops and gain the Line sub-type, and Strike Teams and Pathfinders gain Infiltrate. Limitation: no unit may Advance in the first game turn.

#### Kroot Kindred

Requires a Kroot Shaper as Warlord. Benefit: Kroot Carnivores may be taken in units of up to 30, and Kroot Hounds and Krootox Riders may be taken as Troops. Limitation: no battlesuits other than Stealth Battlesuits, and no Lords of War.

#### Mont'ka

Requires a Commander as Warlord. Benefit: Crisis Battlesuits may be taken as Troops and gain the Line sub-type. Limitation: no Heavy Support choices other than Broadsides, and no Kroot units.

#### Drone Rules

**Drone Support.** A unit that may take drones lists how many in its Options. Drones taken this way are models in that unit: they move and shoot with it and are counted for its Morale checks. If the rest of the unit is destroyed, any remaining drones are removed as casualties.

**Drone Units.** Drones taken as their own unit (Tactical Drones, Sniper Drone Team, Heavy Gun Drones) use these profiles as normal and gain the Sept (X) of their detachment.

**Expendable.** Drones never hold or contest objectives†, and destroying a drone does not count as destroying a model for Morale, Warlord Traits or victory conditions.

**Unit Type.** Drones are Infantry with jet packs and the Drone sub-type†. A Drone may never be a character or join another unit.

## Rules Checklist (†)

These entries rely on 2.0 wording that should be confirmed. Where your book differs, keep the intent and use the book's mechanic.

- Vespid Wings: the Legion Warhawk jump pack rules they copy.
- Jet pack moves in the Shooting phase, and jet packs on heavy battlesuits (Riptide, Ghostkeel, Y'vahra, O'Vesa).
- Heavy units moving and shooting, firing two weapons, and Pinning (Battlesuits).
- Battlesuit type (Riptide, Ghostkeel, Y'vahra, R'varna), the Knight type (Stormsurge), and the Titan type (Ta'unar); Immobile vehicles (Drone Sentry Turret) and fortifications that can move (Tidewalls), including how they are bought.
- Look Out, Sir! and wound allocation to other models (Saviour Protocols, Crisis Bodyguards, Aun'Va).
- A Drone sub-type, Beast sub-type, and models that cannot hold objectives.
- Antigrav, Fast and Open-topped sub-types (Devilfish, Piranha, Hammerhead, Sky Ray).
- Flyer sub-type and flyer Movement (Sun Shark, Razorshark, Barracuda, Orca), and Super-heavy flyers (Tiger Shark, Manta).
- Reaction Allotment, Overwatch from a second unit (Supporting Fire), Return Fire, and extra reactions from Warlord Traits.
- Advancing then shooting (Vior'la, Zephyr's Grace, The Twin Lance).
- Weapon special rules: Sniper, Pinning, Rending, Armourbane, Sunder, Breaching, Skyfire, Interceptor, Hit and Run, firing without line of sight.
- Twin-linked weapons when a battlesuit takes two of the same weapon.
- Grenade effects on the charge bonus (photon grenades), and Outflank.
